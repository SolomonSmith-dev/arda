# Lessons (capture only)

One line per failed judge check, appended by `core/judge.py`. Format:
`- <utc time> | trace_id=<id> | agent=<name> | check=<name> | iteration=<n> | <judge notes>`

Do not edit by hand. Consolidation to under 200 lines happens outside this repo; lessons seen
two or three times are promoted into `CLAUDE.md` or a worker prompt.
